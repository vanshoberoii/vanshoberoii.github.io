Elsevier: Results in Engineering, 2025 | 1st Author | IF: 9.4

Securing Electrical Drive Systems Against Man-in-the-Middle Attacks Using S-Box Optimized AES Encryption

IEEE Access, 2025 | 2nd Author | IF: 3.6
  • Identified lack of encryption in a cloud platform through successful penetration testing
  • Performed a man-in-the-middle attack, leading to successful data modification of critical values in the cloud platform, which was responsible for controlling an electrical drive system
  • Optimized the s-box of the regular AES using Differential Evolution (DE), leading to high randomness and unpredictability in its output further making the DE optimized AES cryptographically stronger
